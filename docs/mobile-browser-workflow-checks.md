Each serialized picker batch gets a fresh estimate with a 1.5-second timeout. The advisory warns when estimated origin headroom is below 50 MiB, usage is at least 80% of quota, or headroom is less than twice total local evidence plus 20 MiB. A batch of at least 20 MiB or a Report with at least 50 MiB of photo/signature evidence also warns even when quota looks ample. These are conservative product thresholds, not measured phone-capacity limits or a promise about required space. Blob sizes and legacy data-string storage costs are counted without decoding photos; the existing signature capture path can encode a live canvas.

The [Storage Standard](https://storage.spec.whatwg.org/#usage-and-quota) defines rough origin estimates, not available device disk space; [WebKit's storage policy](https://webkit.org/blog/14403/updates-to-storage-policy/) documents quota failures and best-effort eviction. Unsupported, denied, malformed and timed-out estimates remain unknown. Neither an estimate nor a successful save guarantees a backup. The app does not request persistent storage, reserve capacity, automatically delete records, clear site data, compress originals or silently submit. Choose fewer photos initially has focus; Add photos anyway is explicit. Cancelling preserves content/evidence, though the existing autosave can refresh the unchanged draft's saved time.

Always-visible New Report/My Reports guidance states that drafts and queued Reports are evictable local copies. Keep originals, avoid clearing this app's site data, keep the page open after failure, free space elsewhere or remove selected photos, and retry saving. An actual quota failure retains the prior saved draft and in-memory edits, with Try saving again. Submission needs a local write too; a failed checkpoint may occur after server acceptance, so the app never infers "not submitted" from a quota error and instead asks the Worker to check My Reports before another attempt.
